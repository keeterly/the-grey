The Grey

A duel of weavers. Three paths to victory.

How to win

  • Ruin — drop your opponent to 0 hearts.
  • Confluence — buy 7 cards from the Aether Flow (one per turn).
  • Dominion — channel 10 or more Aether.

Your turn

Tap a card in your hand to see what you can do — Play it to a Spell Slot, Set it as a Glyph, Cast it, or Channel it for Aether. Pay Aether to advance your Spells; once a spell hits its pip count, it resolves.

Long-press any card to read its full text. The bar at the top tracks your progress on all three win conditions — tap it for a reminder.
